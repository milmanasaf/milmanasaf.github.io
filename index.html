"Journalism is the first draft of history — and every story begins with curiosity."

Welcome to my journalism portfolio. I'm Asaf Milman, a staff writer for both The Campanile and Verde Magazine, the student publications of Palo Alto High School. This portfolio documents my work, growth, and contributions across a range of beats — from local government and education to science, environment, and community issues.

My reporting this semester has taken me from the streets of East Palo Alto to the halls of Paly, covering stories that matter to my community. I believe journalism's power lies in specificity: the right quote, the right source, the right detail that makes a story come alive.
